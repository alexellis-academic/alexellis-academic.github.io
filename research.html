Research interests

Language, media, and community

My interests include language, translation, digital media, mass media, and fan communities, with particular attention to Japanese and Jamaican Creole, online communication, and the social culture of anime fandom.

Current interests

  • Language change, slang, and communication across communities.
  • Translation, transcription, and media.
  • Anime conventions as temporary or autonomous community spaces.
  • Digital fandoms, archives, and informal learning.
  • Youth communication and the use of digital spaces for social support.
